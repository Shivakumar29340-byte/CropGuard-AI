About CropGuard AI

CropGuard AI is a final-year engineering project that helps farmers identify crop diseases from leaf images and keep verifiable farm records.

The problem

Crop diseases are often noticed late, and farmers may not have quick access to an expert. Paper records are also easy to lose or alter.

What the system does

  • Classifies leaf images for tomato, potato, pepper, corn, apple and grape.
  • Shows symptoms, prevention and treatment for the detected disease.
  • Stores detection history for each farmer.
  • Registers a hash of each record on an Ethereum-compatible blockchain (Ganache for local testing) so it can be verified later.
  • Works on the web and on an Android app that shares one backend.

Technology

Web and backend

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5, Flask REST API, SQLite.

AI

Python, TensorFlow/Keras, MobileNetV2, OpenCV, PlantVillage dataset.

Blockchain

Solidity, Ganache, Web3.py, MetaMask if required.

Mobile

Flutter and Dart, connected to the same Flask API.
